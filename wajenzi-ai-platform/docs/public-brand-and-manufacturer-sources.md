# Public Brand and Manufacturer Reference Notes

## Wajenzi Stores branding

The selected Wajenzi Stores wordmark was retrieved from the public Wajenzi Stores brand image result and shows the teal construction-mark icon, **WAJENZI** wordmark, and the strapline “Where builders go.” The source website itself was temporarily unavailable to direct browser retrieval, so the wordmark is used as an attribution-preserving brand reference in the public page rather than being redrawn or altered.

## Manufacturer discovery references

The public manufacturer carousel is intentionally described as a discovery layer rather than an assertion of contractual partnership, reseller status, or endorsement. The official MRM site presents roof and steel roofing solutions, and addresses homeowners plus professional audiences. Crown Paints Kenya lists decorative, waterproofing, adhesives, and specialised coating categories. Both destinations were successfully browser-verified before being retained in the public carousel.
